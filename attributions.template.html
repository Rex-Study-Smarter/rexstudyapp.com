Why there is nothing to review here

Rex is a paid product, and every 3D asset it ships is modified — decimated, re-textured and re-exported — before it reaches a chart. That is incompatible with a NonCommercial or a NoDerivatives licence, so assets carrying those terms are not used, not shipped, and not listed on this page. The build script drops them and prints what it dropped. Everything below is CC BY or CC BY-SA, which permit commercial use and modification with credit.
