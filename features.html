Everything in Rex

One board per class, a tutor that reads it, and the tools around it.

What's inside

Your whole study life, in one app.

It helps you understand. Not just memorize.

Rex Tutor

Ask anything. Rex explains it step by step. Math, science, history, coding. Every subject.

Study Boards

Put notes, charts and ideas on big open boards. Everything syncs. Your session waits where you left it.

Solver

Type an equation. Rex solves it exactly, then shows the working line by line. Tap a line you do not follow, and he explains that step. The algebra runs on your device. The answer is right even with no signal.

Flashcards that come back

Turn a board, a chapter or a lecture into flashcards. Rex schedules them. Easy cards wait for weeks. Hard cards come back in minutes. Your progress follows the card, across decks and devices.

Interactive Charts

Small widgets you play with, not read. Build your own. Publish them. Remix someone else's.

Drawing studio

Work by hand with layers and real brushes. A panel made for thinking on paper. Rex can mark your drawing like a teacher. He points at the line that went wrong.

Recorder

Record the lecture. Get the transcript. Where the language allows, transcription runs on your device. The recording stays yours.

The web, on your board

A browser beside your work. SafeSearch stays on. Ads and trackers stay blocked. Look it up. Keep your place.

Voice Mode

Talk to Rex. He answers out loud, hands free. He works on your board while he talks. Then he steps aside, so your board fills the screen. You decide if he can touch it.

Read aloud

Rex reads it out and follows each sentence. Web pages, PDFs, EPUBs, your notes, even a photo of a worksheet. The voice runs on your device. There is no cap on it.

Real sources

Search real published papers from your board. Keep the good ones next to your notes. A claim in your essay can point at something real.

Bring it all in

Drop in a PDF, a file or a photo. Rex scans it onto the board. Your study material sits next to the work, not in another app.

Books, not just pages

Open an EPUB next to your work. Rex keeps your place. Read aloud works on books too. The book, your notes and your questions live on one board.

Let an AI work on your board

Share one board with one link. Give it to ChatGPT, Claude or any AI you use. It can read that board and help on it. It cannot touch anything else. It logs every change. You can kill the link in a tap.

Works without a signal

Boards, drawings, the solver, read aloud and transcription all work offline. Rex saves to your device first. It syncs when the signal comes back. A bad train ride costs you nothing.

It knows what is due

One reminder a day, at your time. A streak that only counts days you showed up. Home shows what is ready before you go looking.

The Rexer

Live now

Your board, played back as a lecture.

Everything on a board is material. The Rexer turns that pile into a taught lesson. Out loud. One thing at a time. On the board you already built.

01

Tap Rexer

It sits with your widgets. Nothing to set up. Nothing to export. No render to wait for.

02

Rex reads the board

Your notes, PDFs, scans, drawings, charts, kept pages and recordings. Rex reads each one in full. A big board gets taught, not summarised.

03

The board becomes a stage

Everything goes dark. Rex lights up one item at a time and talks over it in his own voice. Captions included. Jump around the running order. Stop and ask about the thing on screen.

04

Steer it, and keep it

Stop him any time. Ask for that part again, another way. The lecture leaves notes behind on the board as it goes. The end card shows where it all came from: your work, your recording, and the parts Rex added. Save it as a video, or send it to someone.

The board you give it is the lecture you get.

The Rexer never invents your lesson. It teaches what is on the board, in the order the board suggests. It gives more time to what you worked on. A better lecture does not need a better prompt. It needs a better board.

Six things that change the result.

Put something real on it A near empty board has nothing to teach from. Notes in your own words are the best material. They are the only part that is truly yours.
Record the lesson on the board A recording becomes the spine. The lecture then follows the order of the class. Not the order things landed on the canvas.
One board per lesson The board's name becomes the title of the lecture. A board that's three subjects deep gets taught as three subjects.
Lay it out in reading order Rex walks the board the way you would read it, top to bottom, page by page. Moving a tile up moves it earlier in the lecture.
Actually work in it Pages you worked on get more lecture time. The two pages you were stuck on get taught properly, not one line each. That record stays on your device.
Add the thing you couldn't explain Drop in the PDF, the scanned worksheet, the page you found. Rex labels anything he covers for you as his. You see exactly where your own understanding stopped.

Every stop shows where it came from: your notepad, your recording, or Rex filling a gap. And if you're offline, out of your daily allowance, or on a board Rex can't make sense of, you still get something. He reads the board back to you in its own words, marked as a read through, so a read through is never passed off as a lecture.
