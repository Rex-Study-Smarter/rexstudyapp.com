The Rexer

Coming in the next update

Your board, played back as a lecture.

Everything you put on a board is material. The Rexer turns that pile into a taught lesson, out loud, one thing at a time, on the board you already built.

01

Tap Rexer

It sits on the same button you add widgets with. Nothing to set up, nothing to export, no render to wait through.

02

Rex reads the board

Your notes, PDFs, scans, drawings, charts, the pages you kept and the lecture you recorded. Each one is read in full rather than skimmed, so a big board gets taught, not summarised.

03

The board becomes a stage

Everything goes dark and Rex lights up one item at a time, talking over it in his own voice, with captions, a running order you can jump around in, and a way to stop and ask him about the thing on screen.

04

Keep the lecture

The end card shows what it was built from: how many stops came from your own work, how many from your recording, and how many Rex added where your notes stopped. Save it as a video or send it to someone.

The board you give it is the lecture you get.

The Rexer never invents your lesson. It teaches what is on the board, in the order the board suggests, weighted by what you actually spent the session on. So the way to get a better lecture is not a better prompt. It's a better board.

Six things that change the result.

Put something real on it A near empty board has nothing to teach from. Notes in your own words are the strongest material Rex has, because they are the only part that is actually yours.
Record the lesson on the board When there's a recording, it becomes the spine. The lecture then follows the order the class was taught in, instead of the order things happened to land on the canvas.
One board per lesson The board's name becomes the title of the lecture. A board that's three subjects deep gets taught as three subjects.
Lay it out in reading order Rex walks the board the way you would read it, top to bottom, page by page. Moving a tile up moves it earlier in the lecture.
Actually work in it What you opened and sat with gets more of the lecture's time, so the two pages you were stuck on get taught properly instead of getting a line each. That record of your session stays on your device.
Add the thing you couldn't explain Drop in the PDF, the scanned worksheet, the page you found. Anything Rex has to cover for you is labelled as his, so you can see exactly where your own understanding ran out.

Every stop is labelled with where it came from: your notepad, your recording, or Rex filling a gap. And if you're offline, out of your daily allowance, or on a board Rex can't make sense of, you still get something: he reads the board back to you in its own words, marked as a read through, so a read through is never passed off as a lecture.

Questions

Straight answers.

The stuff people ask before they download.

Is Rex really free?

Yes. The tutor, study boards, whiteboard, notes, charts and read aloud are all on the free plan, with no credit card and no trial timer. Paid plans add capacity and advanced tools. They don't unlock the basics.

What devices does Rex run on?

Rex is on iPhone and iPad today. Android is in the works.

Is my voice recorded or sent anywhere?

Read aloud and dictation both run on your device, and dictation audio is discarded the moment it becomes text. A recording you deliberately save is different: it is a file you are keeping, so the transcript syncs to your account and the audio stays on your device unless you choose to upload it. The full detail is in our privacy policy.

Can I make my own charts?

You can. Build one in the app with quiz modes or Frame Studio, or import your own self-contained HTML file. The full guide and starter template are in the chart docs.

What is the Rexer?

It plays your board back to you as a spoken lecture. Rex reads everything on the board, your notes, PDFs, scans, drawings, charts and any lecture you recorded there, then darkens the board and lights up one item at a time while he teaches it. You can jump around the running order, stop and ask him about whatever is on screen, and save the whole thing as a video at the end. Every stop says where it came from, so you can always tell your own work from the parts Rex filled in. It arrives in the next update.

How do I get a better lecture out of the Rexer?

Give it a better board. It teaches what is actually there, in the order the board suggests, and it gives more time to the things you spent the session in. So: keep one board per lesson, lay it out in reading order, write your notes in your own words, and record the class on the board when you can. A recording becomes the spine of the lecture, so it follows the order the class was taught in rather than the order tiles landed on the canvas.

What is Voice Mode?

A hands free conversation with Rex. You talk, he answers out loud, and he can work on your board while he explains, pulling up a chart or writing something down as he talks. When he touches the board he steps aside so you can see it, and shrinks to a chip in the corner. Whether he is allowed to change the board is a switch you control, and you can end the session at any time.

Can Rex read a PDF or a photo out loud?

Yes. Read aloud handles web pages, PDFs, your own notes, and images, including a photo of a worksheet or a scanned page, by reading the text out of the picture first. Rex highlights the sentence he is on as he goes, and the voice is generated on your device.

What is the difference between monthly and annual Pro?

Nothing except how you pay. Pro is $12.99 a month, or $99.99 for a full year billed once. Both plans include every tool and the same 300,000 daily tutor tokens.

What is an AI token, and will I run out?

Tokens are how the tutor's usage is measured: roughly speaking, a token is about three quarters of a word, counting both your question and Rex's answer. Free gets 50,000 a day, Pro 300,000, and the count resets every day. Most study sessions do not come close. If you do run out, the tutor pauses until tomorrow and everything else in Rex keeps working.

Can I use my own AI provider?

Yes. You can connect your own OpenRouter account, and the tutor will answer through a model you choose and pay for instead of Rex's, without passing through our servers. Free models are available there, so it is a way to keep going past the daily allowance without a Rex subscription.

What happens to my work if I cancel?

Your boards, notes and recordings stay exactly where they are. You drop back to the free plan's daily allowance, but nothing is deleted for cancelling.
